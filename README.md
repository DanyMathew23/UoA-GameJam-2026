# UoA-GameJam-2026
Traces of the Past: Colachel 1741

Team: Solo
Engine: Unity (C#)
Genre: Narrative historical strategy
Theme: Traces of the Past

Historical Basis

This game is based on the Battle of Colachel (1741), fought between the Kingdom of Travancore under Maharaja Marthanda Varma and the Dutch East India Company (VOC). It is historically significant as one of the earliest recorded defeats of a European colonial power by an Asian kingdom in open battle.

Historical sources include:

VOC military records
Travancore state chronicles
Physical remains and fortifications at Colachel

Gameplay:

The player takes the role of a Travancore commander and progresses through key stages of the battle by making strategic decisions. The game emphasizes:
Terrain advantage
Morale and leadership

Asymmetry between the indigenous and colonial forces:
The outcome follows historical events rather than alternate history.

Controls:
Mouse click on UI button to advance decisions
